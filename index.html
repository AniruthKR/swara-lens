Swara notation

Dot above = tara sthayi, dot below = mandra sthayi. ~ kampita, ≈ andolita, / \ jaru up or down into the note, ↗ ↘ slide inside a note, * quick touch of a neighbouring swara, | pause. Tap a note to hear it.

How to read this

  • Swaras are placed on a just-intonation grid above your Sa. Where R2 and G1, or R3 and G2, share a position, the shared name is used (R2, G2, D2, N2).
  • Gamakas are detected with simple pitch-movement rules, so treat them as hints, not as a transcription a guru would sign off.
  • The raga suggestion looks at which swaras are used and for how long. It does not hear characteristic phrases, so ragas with identical notes are grouped together.
